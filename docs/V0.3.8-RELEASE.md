# DeenPath 0.3.8 (11)

Update over the existing app to preserve local data. The project folder remains SalahGuide as requested.

- Timed-only prayer Enforcer. App-opening detection and Usage Access have been removed.
- Defaults: 15 minutes early, 10 minutes in the middle, 1 minute in the final phase. Change each interval, phase boundary and snooze duration in Settings > Reminders & sound. For a 5–6 AM window, the default phase boundaries round to 5:25 and 5:45. No alert is scheduled at or after the ending time.
- Qaza reminders are suppressed, including legacy queued end-of-window events and snoozes extending beyond the prayer window. Manual Qaza cards/history remain available. Expired Enforcers close and their notifications expire.
- Reliable prayer alarms use OS alarm-clock scheduling plus a visible background timer service. The notification opens DeenPath and has an explicit Turn off Enforcer action. The service starts on normal app launch, app update and boot when enabled. Android Force stop suspends delivery until DeenPath is reopened; OEM restrictions can still affect delivery.
- Full-screen Enforcer with a pinned Close control, scrollable readings, I Prayed and snooze. Opening an app itself does not trigger it.
- Different attributed ayats and authentic hadith excerpts rotate across delivered Enforcers, avoiding consecutive duplicates. Choose a mixed theme, hope/mercy, forgiveness/repentance, or prayer/consistency. English and Urdu are available together. Hadith meanings are labelled as summaries and Arabic as excerpts; source references are shown.
- About & Contact introduces the app's purpose and Syed Anas Ali Askari, with a reviewable email draft to syedanasaliaskarivu@gmail.com.
- Home spacing, circular person avatar and full Hijri month names; local moon-sighting adjustment stays configurable. Nafl remains in Routines, Qaza in Prayer. Progress has Gardens. Routine attachments stay with their cards, Secrets supports deletion, Qibla location detection and Guidance UI are improved. Production test controls and editable API URL are removed.

Hosting was deferred by the owner. The approved public endpoint https://lendafile.com/salahguard/api.php is configured but the PHP/backend deployment and live database checks are pending; the endpoint returned 404. Offline features remain available. Existing nonempty API configuration is preserved. No hosting files or live database were modified. Do not put database credentials in the app.

See V0.3.8-TESTING.md for verification and device-specific limits.
