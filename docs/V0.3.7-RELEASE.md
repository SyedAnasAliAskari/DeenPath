# DeenPath 0.3.7

Android version code 10. Install over the existing app; retain app storage and the SalahGuide folder name.

## Prayer alarms and Enforcer

- Adaptive reminders: every 30 minutes early, every 10 minutes midway, every 5 minutes late, and every minute in the final 10 minutes. The final ten-minute phase takes priority over percentage thresholds, including short windows. One missed-prayer notification follows the ending boundary. A real prayer completion cancels its remaining alerts.
- Choose Custom to retain explicit once-only or 1/5/10/15/30/60-minute stage intervals. Existing warning/strict thresholds remain configurable. Silent mode and chosen sounds remain supported.
- Reliable prayer alarms use Android AlarmClock scheduling through the existing single-alarm dispatcher. The next reminder appears in Android’s next-alarm indicator. Frequent wake-ups use more battery. Turn off this option to use the ordinary idle-alarm path.
- Prayer channels have alarm audio usage and high importance, including early/midway stages. Channel migration retains explicit user importance/blocking and sound choices; it does not bypass disabled channels or Do Not Disturb. Locked-screen notifications use the alarm category and the Android 15 PendingIntent creator opt-in.
- Setup shows app-specific background battery access and opens Android’s permission dialog when needed.
- Pop-ups and Wake Screen are separate visible switches. On the user’s phone, Android permissions were already granted but both app switches were off; they were enabled through the normal UI as requested.
- Snooze blocks dense repeats until its deadline. Close dismisses the current event; later reminders remain scheduled. An overdue batch delivers only its latest relevant event per route rather than a burst of stale overlays.
- Boot, package replacement and clock changes restore the queue directly from the receiver, with WorkManager as an error fallback. Previous-day active windows are included when rebuilding, preserving Isha reminders across midnight. Private reminder deep links now reach their gated destination.

## Android limits

Closing the app or normal process death leaves native alarms active. Android Force stop is different: Android 15 cancels the app’s pending intents until the user launches/interacts with it again. Do not use Force stop if you want alarms to continue. Exact-alarm, notification, display-over-other-apps and full-screen permissions must remain allowed; the phone’s battery/OEM settings may add restrictions.

Primary Android guidance: [exact alarms](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/alarms), [time-sensitive notifications](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/time-sensitive), [Android 15 PendingIntent launch rules](https://developer.android.com/guide/components/activities/background-starts), [Force-stop behavior](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/15/behavior-changes-all).
